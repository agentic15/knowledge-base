Adaptive Insights is a cloud-based financial planning and analysis platform acquired by Workday that enables mid-market and enterprise companies to perform budgeting, planning, forecasting, and reporting. The platform emphasizes ease of use and speed of implementation compared to traditional EPM solutions.

- **Planning Cycle Management** — Structured process for annual and rolling planning
- **User-friendly Interface** — Reduced learning curve vs. enterprise planning tools
- **Flexible Modeling** — Quick model changes without complex coding
- **Predictive Analytics** — Machine learning-based forecasting recommendations
- **Mobile Planning** — Planning and approval workflows on mobile devices
- **Reporting & Analysis** — Self-service reporting and analysis tools

Adaptive Insights provides a cloud platform that connects to GL systems and operational data sources. Users build financial models using a simplified interface that doesn't require programming. Once models are created, the platform manages planning cycles where stakeholders submit budget and forecast assumptions. Machine learning analyzes historical patterns to recommend forecast values. Approval workflows route plans through designated approvers. Dashboards track actual results against budget and forecast, showing variance analysis. The platform supports sensitivity analysis and scenario modeling to test strategic assumptions. Real-time reporting enables rapid cycle close and results communication.

- Mid-market and enterprise budget and planning operations
- Rapid FP&A tool implementation for smaller organizations
- Rolling forecast management with predictive analytics
- Integrated planning across finance, sales, and HR
- Executive scenario analysis and decision support

| Advantage | Disadvantage |
|-----------|--------------|
| Faster implementation than traditional EPM solutions | Less powerful for highly complex scenarios |
| Easier to learn and use for business users | Limited customization vs. more flexible platforms |
| Good balance of functionality and ease of use | May outgrow platform capabilities over time |
| Reasonable cost for mid-market | Integration with non-standard systems may be limited |
