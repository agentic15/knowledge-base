Vertex AI Model Garden provides curated collection of pre-trained models and foundation models available for deployment. This marketplace accelerates development by offering ready-to-use models across vision, language, and multimodal tasks.

- **Model Curation** — expert selection of quality models and solutions
- **Foundation Models** — large pre-trained models adaptable to specific tasks
- **Model Cards** — comprehensive documentation of capabilities and limitations
- **Quick Deployment** — minimal configuration for model launching
- **Fine-tuning Readiness** — pre-trained models ready for task-specific customization


Model Garden organizes models by task, domain, and provider. Each model includes detailed cards describing architecture, performance, limitations, and ethical considerations. Models can be deployed directly to Vertex AI endpoints with minimal configuration. Fine-tuning options enable customization for specific use cases. Models range from general-purpose foundation models to specialized domain models. Versioning enables tracking updates and pinning to specific versions.

- Rapid model discovery for specific problems
- Accelerating prototyping and proof-of-concepts
- Finding pre-trained models to fine-tune
- Comparing model options for tasks
- Exploring state-of-the-art solutions
- Building AI applications quickly

| Advantage | Disadvantage |
|-----------|--------------|
| Curated selection saves research time | Limited to available models |
| Detailed model cards guide decisions | Not all open-source models included |
| Quick deployment simplicity | Standardized configurations less flexible |
| Quality assurance on models | Model support dependent on Google |
| Foundation models for customization | Pricing implications of different models |
